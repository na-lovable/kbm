# Metro Vertiport Alpha Battery Swap Station (BSS-600 Compact)

The **Metro Vertiport Alpha Battery Swap Station** is a scaled-down variant of the BSS-600 system, engineered for high-density urban environments. It services medium-payload delivery UAVs such as the [SwiftPack X-4 Urban Delivery Drone](../../fleet/urban-courier/swiftpack-x4-delivery-uav.md).

## Operational Highlights
- **Swap Cycle Time:** 95 seconds (single-bay operation)
- **Robotic Arm:** 4-Axis lightweight actuator with ±0.3 mm repeatability
- **Battery Compatibility:** Integrated with the [48V Solid-State Lithium Battery Pack](../../power/energy-storage/solid-state-lithium-pack-48v.md)
- **Footprint:** 2 m × 2 m floor impact, suitable for rooftop installations with load-capacity up to 5,000 kg

## Integration & Deployment
The compact station is linked to the [Metro Vertiport Alpha (Downtown Hub)](../hubs/metro-rooftop-vertiport-alpha.md) and acts as a spoke node in the city-wide battery-swap network, enabling swift battery changes for high-frequency delivery loops.
