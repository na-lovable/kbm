# Skyport Central Air Freight Fulfillment Hub

**Skyport Central (Hub ID: SKY-01-CENTRAL)** is the flagship regional autonomous logistics node. Spanning 45,000 square meters on the metropolitan periphery, it combines high-speed robotic parcel sortation, automated drone launch bays, heavy MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Overhaul) hangars, and central UTM dispatch command.

---

## 2. Fleet Deployment & Hangars

Skyport Central hosts the primary operations and depot-level maintenance for:
- Regional heavy-lift multicopters: [SkyLift H-80 Heavy Cargo Drone](../../fleet/heavy-lift/skylift-h80-cargo-drone.md)
- Long-endurance inter-city aircraft: [RotorWing V-200 Hybrid Cargo Cruiser](../../fleet/heavy-lift/rotorwing-v200-hybrid.md)

All charging operations are routed through the facility's dedicated battery bank powered by the [Autonomous Robotic Battery Swap Station (BSS-600)](../charging/automated-battery-swap-station.md).

---

## 3. Air Traffic Control & Regulatory Adherence

The hub's operations control center acts as the primary network node for monitoring flight corridors established under the [BVLOS Low-Altitude Corridor Navigation Procedure](../../operations/flight-corridors/bvlos-corridor-nav-procedure.md).

All aircraft movements, ADS-B Out broadcasts, and telemetry logging conform to the strict mandates of the [FAA Part 108 BVLOS Regulatory Compliance Framework](../../safety/regulations/faa-part-108-bvlos-compliance.md).
